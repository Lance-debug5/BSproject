LittleBotsBigCity

Welcome

Welcome to LittleBotsBigCity! This is where small figuress meet the big apple—combining forced-perspective outdoor toy photography with custom web development and interactive JavaScript projects.

A lil about me

My real name is Shane, though I also go by Lance—either works! I'm a Computer Information Systems major in my second-to-last semester. When I'm not coding or exploring what's next after graduation, I take Transformers and collectible figures out into parks, trails, and cityscapes to photograph them in real-world environments.

Goals

I'm aiming to grow LittleBotsBigplanet as both an outdoor figure photography archive and a showcase for my programming and web development skills using JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and Linux.
